Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026. Short version: Momo has no account, no ads and no tracking across apps. Your progress stays on your phone. We collect a few anonymous usage numbers, which you can switch off.

Who we are

Momo is made by ericora ("we"). Contact: momo.focus.support@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Your toys, Buttons, borrowed and bought items, session history, settings and streaks are stored on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Anonymous usage numbers (can be turned off)

To learn whether people use Momo and where it can be better, the app sends a few events to PostHog, a product analytics service: the app was opened, a session started or ended (and how long), which toy was tried, applied, borrowed or bought. Each phone gets a random number so we can tell one install from another. We do not send your name, email, contact details, location, advertising identifier or anything you type, and the events are not linked to who you are. You can turn this off at any time in Settings, Help improve Momo; nothing is sent after that. Data is processed by PostHog on servers in the United States and kept for up to one year.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use Settings, Send feedback, we receive the message you write, the type you picked, the toy you were on, and your email only if you typed one. We use it to read and answer feedback and to decide what to build. Please do not include private information. You can ask us to delete a message you sent.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app learns from Apple which items you own so it can unlock them and let you restore them.

Notifications and your lock screen

Momo can show a reminder, a message when a session ends, and a countdown on the lock screen and Dynamic Island. These are created on your phone; they do not use a server, and you can turn them off in iPhone Settings.

Children

Momo is for everyone and is not marketed to children as a "kids app". We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13: the usage numbers are anonymous, and the only thing a person can send us themselves is feedback, which they can leave out an email from. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off usage numbers, notifications and live activities, delete the app, and email us to ask what we hold about a feedback message you sent or to have it deleted. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete personal information; we will honour them within a reasonable time.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for meaningful changes tell you in the app.
