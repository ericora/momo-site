Squish it, don't scroll it.

Momo is a calm little toy for your phone: a soft felt dog to squeeze, beads to roll, a spinner to flick, a pen to click. Play any time. When you want to stay with something, tap the time and Momo keeps you company, with a countdown on your lock screen and no guilt if it doesn't go to plan.

What you can do

  • Squeeze, spin, click and pop toys made of felt, foam and wood.
  • Start a gentle session (5 to 120 minutes) and earn Buttons for finishing it.
  • Borrow new toys with Buttons, or keep the ones you love forever.
  • Open Momo from another app with a Shortcut, so a toy meets you before the scroll does.

Made with care

Momo has no ads and no account. Your progress stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy, or get in touch.
