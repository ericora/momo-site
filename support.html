Common questions

I bought something and it is gone after a new phone or reinstall.

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple account.

A toy I borrowed went away.

Borrowed toys last 7 days and then go back on the shelf. Nothing is lost: borrow it again with Buttons, or buy it to keep it forever. Your progress with that toy is kept.

My session ended when I switched apps.

If you leave Momo for another app for more than 15 seconds, the session ends gently and you can start again. Locking your phone does not count as leaving.

I do not see the countdown on my lock screen.

Turn on Live Activities for Momo in the iPhone Settings app, under Momo. It needs iOS 16.2 or newer.

How do I open Momo from another app?

In the Shortcuts app, make an automation "When I open [an app]" and add the action "Open URL" with momo://play. Settings in Momo can copy a link for you.

Can I delete my data?

Your progress lives only on your phone: delete the app to remove it. For anonymous usage numbers and any feedback you sent, email us and we will delete what we can find, see the privacy policy.
